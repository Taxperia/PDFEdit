# Code of conduct

PDFEdit welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews and other project-managed community spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive and specific.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking the people behind them.
- Accept good-faith feedback and help newcomers participate.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution and licensing.
- Keep conversations relevant and use English where possible so project discussions remain accessible to the wider community.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, spam and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

For conduct concerns, use a private contact method listed on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Taxperia). If no private route is available, request a contact channel without naming affected people or exposing sensitive details in a public issue. GitHub's own reporting and blocking tools remain available for platform-level abuse. Technical vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer may remove or edit harmful content, ask for a change in behavior, restrict participation, or block accounts depending on the severity and repetition of the behavior. Reports should be handled with care and shared only as needed to address the concern. There is no promise of absolute confidentiality or a fixed response deadline.
